Sonora is installed and already working

Pin the toolbar icon and Sonora controls every audible tab right away — nothing else to set up. The free companion app for macOS is optional: it adds your Bluetooth headset's battery level to the cockpit.

Download HeadsetCompanion (free)

Three steps

  1. Open the DMG and drag HeadsetCompanion to Applications.
  2. Right-click the app → Open → Open. This is needed once, because the app is free and not notarised by Apple.
  3. That's it — this page notices the app the moment it starts.
