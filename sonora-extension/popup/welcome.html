Headset controls

Swipe to the next thing that's playing

Every tab making noise is one swipe from the next — the one you land on starts, the one you left pauses. On a WH-1000XM4 these are the right-panel gestures; any headset that sends the same commands works the same way.

Swipe forward

Next source

Starts the next audible tab. The one you were on pauses.

Swipe back

Previous source

The same, the other way. The list of tabs wraps around.

Swipe and hold

Jump 10 seconds

Hold forward to skip ahead, back to go back. Live streams ignore it.

Double-tap

Play or pause

Toggles the source Sonora is controlling. It keeps its place.

These gestures need HeadsetCompanion. Your headset talks to macOS, not to Chrome — the companion app below is what passes the commands along. Until it's running, the buttons do whatever they did before.

Volume is still macOS. Swipe up or down and you're moving the system level, as always. Sonora has its own slider in the cockpit.

If a gesture goes to the wrong app, something else claimed the media keys — starting playback in another app does that. The cockpit's top line names who holds them: keys: Sonora. Press again and Sonora takes them back.

Click the puzzle piece in Chrome's toolbar, then the pin beside Sonora. The icon shows a count of the tabs currently playing.

Every audible tab

Sources are listed in tab order, with the one you're controlling highlighted. Click any of them to move the signal there.

Send audio elsewhere

Route to… picks the output device for the controlled tab, so a stream can go to the headset while the rest of the machine doesn't.

Companion app · macOS

HeadsetCompanion

A small menu-bar app. It reads your headset's real battery level and passes the headset's buttons through to Sonora. Free, open source, and optional — the cockpit works without it.

Download HeadsetCompanion

Apple Silicon · macOS 13 or later

  1. Open the DMG and drag HeadsetCompanion to Applications.
  2. Right-click it → Open → Open. Once only: the app is free and unsigned, so Gatekeeper asks the first time.
  3. Watch the bar at the top of this page — it turns lime the moment the app starts serving.

Nothing leaves your machine. The app serves on 127.0.0.1:8745 and only Sonora reads it. No account, no telemetry, no network calls.
